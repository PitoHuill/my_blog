## Tools Should Reduce Friction

Tools are not meant to accelerate everything; they are meant to reduce pointless friction.

When a system needs constant maintenance, migration, and reconfiguration, attention moves away from the real problem. For personal projects, simplicity often means a longer life.

## The Strength of Small Systems

A system that is small enough is often easier to understand and maintain, and more able to accompany a long-term question to its end.

- Clear boundaries make problems easier to locate.
- Fewer dependencies lower the cost of future migration.
- Short daily routines are easier to turn into habits.

Writing, reading, and project management can all begin with this restraint.
