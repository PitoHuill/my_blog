## Clear Does Not Mean Simple

Clarity is not about making a problem simpler; it is about making its structure visible.

Complex problems can remain complex, but readers need to know where they are in an argument and how this part relates to its conclusion.

## A Reliable Order of Expression

State the problem first, then offer a judgment, and finally show the reasoning. This helps readers build their own understanding more than a pile of conclusions does.

> A good explanation does not spare readers from thinking; it makes the path of thought possible to follow.

After writing, revisit the headings, paragraph openings, and transition sentences. They usually reveal the places where the structure is least clear.
