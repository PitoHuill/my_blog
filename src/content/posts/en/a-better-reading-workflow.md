## Start with a Question

The point of reading is not the number of books completed, but whether it builds a library of your own questions.

Writing down a question you want to resolve before reading helps you decide which passages deserve annotation. It also reduces indiscriminate saving for something that might be useful someday.

## Separate Input from Organization

While reading, make only light marks; do not rush to turn everything into finished notes. Organizing and expressing require a different kind of attention. Once they are separated, both become easier.

## Keep a Gentle Review Rhythm

Reviewing a small number of excerpts each week is more reliable than occasionally attempting one enormous clean-up.

What remains in the end need not be a complete summary. It can be a question, an explanation in your own words, and a direction for the next exploration.
